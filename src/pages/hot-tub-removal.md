<p class="closer">We remove hot tubs and spas, working or not. If it won't fit through the gate in one piece, we take it apart and haul it away.</p>
<p class="closer">To get a price, text a photo of the tub and a second photo of the route from the tub to the street. Gates, steps, and tight side yards affect the work, along with weight. See <a href="/junk-removal-cost/">how junk removal is priced</a>.</p>
<p class="closer">Before we arrive, turn the power off at the breaker. If the tub still holds water, let us know when you text.</p>
<p class="closer">In <a href="/tarzana/">Tarzana</a> and <a href="/encino/">Encino</a>, spas are often behind the main house, past a pool house or a long side yard. In <a href="/woodland-hills/">Woodland Hills</a>, patio steps on a slope can make the carry harder. We plan for it. A tub that's still in good shape goes to donation, and the rest is recycled for metal or disposed of properly.</p>
