<p class="closer">A dead spa on the patio is one of our regular calls. We take hot tubs, working or not, and we take them apart when they won't fit through the gate in one piece.</p>
<p class="closer">Text a photo of the tub and a second photo of the route from the tub to the street. Side gates, steps, and fences set the work more than the tub does. Weight and complexity are two of the three things that <a href="/junk-removal-cost/">set the price</a>.</p>
<p class="closer">Turn the power off at the breaker before we arrive. If the tub still holds water, tell us when you text.</p>
<p class="closer">The lots in <a href="/tarzana/">Tarzana</a> and <a href="/encino/">Encino</a> often put the spa behind the main house, past a pool house or a long side yard. In <a href="/woodland-hills/">Woodland Hills</a>, patio steps on a slope make the carry harder. A working tub that is still good goes to donation. The rest gets recycled for metal or disposed of properly.</p>
