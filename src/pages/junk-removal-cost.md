<p class="closer">Every job is priced from a photo, so you know the cost before we arrive. We don't use a flat rate card because two piles that look alike can take very different work. Three things affect your price: distance, rush, and weight and complexity.</p>
<div class="answers">
<h3>Distance</h3>
<p class="closer">How far we drive to you. <a href="/woodland-hills/">Woodland Hills</a>, <a href="/tarzana/">Tarzana</a>, <a href="/reseda/">Reseda</a>, <a href="/encino/">Encino</a>, and <a href="/sherman-oaks/">Sherman Oaks</a> are the neighborhoods we work most. A job farther out in the San Fernando Valley is priced for the drive.</p>
<h3>Rush</h3>
<p class="closer">Same day or next day is our normal pace in most cases. If you need a fixed time on short notice, the price goes up. Tell us your deadline when you text.</p>
<h3>Weight and complexity</h3>
<p class="closer">Heavy loads like concrete, dirt, and roofing cost more to haul and dump, so text us what you have before you count on a number. Complexity covers anything beyond lifting: stairs, a long carry from a back house, gates, a tight hillside driveway, or a hot tub or shed that has to be taken apart first.</p>
</div>
<p class="closer">Put the pile and the path to it in your photo. If the job is big or complex, we come look in person the same day and quote you there. Either way, we name the price up front.</p>
<p class="closer">Some items are not part of any quote. See <a href="/what-we-dont-take/">what we can't take</a> before you text.</p>
